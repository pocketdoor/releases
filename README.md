# Pocket Door

Your Android phone's notifications, texts, calls, photos and files on your Windows PC, over your own Wi-Fi.
No account, no servers: your phone and your PC talk only to each other, encrypted.

## Get it

Both downloads are on the [latest release](https://github.com/arvindmaiya/pocketdoor-releases/releases/latest).

**On your Windows PC:** download **PocketDoor.exe** and run it. Windows may say it protected your PC: click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. Pocket Door installs itself, shows a code to scan, and keeps itself up to date.

**On your Android phone:** open the release page on your phone, download **PocketDoor.apk** and open it. Allow your
browser to install apps when Android asks. Then scan the code on your PC.

If Android says a setting is restricted when you turn on notification access or automatic copy: open Android's
**Settings › Apps › Pocket Door**, tap **⋮ › Allow restricted settings**, and try again.

Both apps check this page for new versions and update themselves.
